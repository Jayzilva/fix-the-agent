# Custom domain SSL certificate stuck on 'pending'

Article ID: KB-016 | Category: technical | Last updated: 2026-08-01

## Summary

Certificates are issued once the CNAME points to edge.nimbus.example. Remove conflicting CAA records, then click Retry issuance under Domains.

## Symptoms

You may see this when the SSL certificate for our custom domain has been stuck on 'pending' and the site shows a certificate warning.

## Common question

Customers often ask how long does it take for an SSL certificate to be issued for a custom domain.

## Resolution

1. Certificates are issued once the CNAME points to edge.nimbus.example.

2. Remove conflicting CAA records, then click Retry issuance under Domains.
