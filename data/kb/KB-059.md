# Data residency and compliance

Article ID: KB-059 | Category: other | Last updated: 2026-08-15

## Summary

Nimbus Cloud is SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 certified. Data residency is available in the US and EU regions.

## Resolution

1. Nimbus Cloud is SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 certified.

2. Data residency is available in the US and EU regions.
