# Updating your payment method and declined cards

Article ID: KB-004 | Category: billing | Last updated: 2026-08-15

## Summary

Go to Billing > Payment methods, add the new card, then mark it as default. Declines with code 'do_not_honor' come from the bank; ask the bank to allow recurring charges from Nimbus Cloud.

## Symptoms

You may see this when my card keeps getting declined when I try to pay and the payment page shows an error.

## Common question

Customers often ask how do I change the credit card we pay with.

## Resolution

1. Go to Billing > Payment methods, add the new card, then mark it as default.

2. Declines with code 'do_not_honor' come from the bank; ask the bank to allow recurring charges from Nimbus Cloud.
