# Refund policy

Article ID: KB-003 | Category: billing | Last updated: 2026-08-17

## Summary

Monthly plans can be refunded within 14 days of the charge. Annual plans can be refunded within 30 days of the charge. Free plans have no charges to refund, and Enterprise contracts are refunded only through the account manager.

## Symptoms

You may see this when I want my money back for the last payment because we no longer need the service.

## Common question

Customers often ask how does your refund policy work and am I eligible for a refund.

## Resolution

1. Monthly plans can be refunded within 14 days of the charge.

2. Annual plans can be refunded within 30 days of the charge.

3. Free plans have no charges to refund, and Enterprise contracts are refunded only through the account manager.
