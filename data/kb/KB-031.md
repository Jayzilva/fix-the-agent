# Cron jobs not running

Article ID: KB-031 | Category: technical | Last updated: 2026-08-09

## Summary

Cron schedules use UTC and standard five-field syntax. Jobs pause automatically after 5 consecutive failures; fix the error and click Resume under Jobs.

## Symptoms

You may see this when our scheduled cron jobs stopped running and no executions show in the dashboard.

## Common question

Customers often ask how do I schedule a cron job.

## Resolution

1. Cron schedules use UTC and standard five-field syntax.

2. Jobs pause automatically after 5 consecutive failures; fix the error and click Resume under Jobs.
