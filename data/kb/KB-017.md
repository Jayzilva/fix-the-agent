# Webhook deliveries failing with 401

Article ID: KB-017 | Category: technical | Last updated: 2026-08-02

## Summary

Webhooks are signed with the endpoint's signing secret. A 401 means your receiver is validating with an old secret; copy the current secret from Webhooks > Endpoint and redeploy the receiver.

## Symptoms

You may see this when all our webhook deliveries are failing with HTTP 401 Unauthorized.

## Common question

Customers often ask how do webhook signatures work.

## Resolution

1. Webhooks are signed with the endpoint's signing secret.

2. A 401 means your receiver is validating with an old secret; copy the current secret from Webhooks > Endpoint and redeploy the receiver.
