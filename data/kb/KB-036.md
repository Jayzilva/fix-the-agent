# Data export jobs stuck

Article ID: KB-036 | Category: technical | Last updated: 2026-08-12

## Summary

Exports run in a queue; jobs stuck over 2 hours can be cancelled and restarted from Settings > Export. Very large exports should be split by project.

## Symptoms

You may see this when our data export job has been stuck at 0 percent for hours.

## Common question

Customers often ask how do I export all of our project data.

## Resolution

1. Exports run in a queue; jobs stuck over 2 hours can be cancelled and restarted from Settings > Export.

2. Very large exports should be split by project.
