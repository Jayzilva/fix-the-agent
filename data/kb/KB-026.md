# Container image pull errors (ImagePullBackOff)

Article ID: KB-026 | Category: technical | Last updated: 2026-08-02

## Summary

Add the registry credentials under Project > Registries and reference them in the service. ImagePullBackOff usually means the tag does not exist or the credentials expired.

## Symptoms

You may see this when our service is stuck in ImagePullBackOff and the new version never starts.

## Common question

Customers often ask how do I use a private container registry.

## Resolution

1. Add the registry credentials under Project > Registries and reference them in the service.

2. ImagePullBackOff usually means the tag does not exist or the credentials expired.
