# Autoscaling not triggering

Article ID: KB-025 | Category: technical | Last updated: 2026-08-12

## Summary

Autoscaling uses the average CPU across instances over 5 minutes. Make sure max instances is above the current count and that the service is not pinned to a fixed size.

## Symptoms

You may see this when autoscaling is not adding instances even though CPU is at 100 percent.

## Common question

Customers often ask how does autoscaling decide when to add instances.

## Resolution

1. Autoscaling uses the average CPU across instances over 5 minutes.

2. Make sure max instances is above the current count and that the service is not pinned to a fixed size.
