# Taxes and VAT IDs on invoices

Article ID: KB-007 | Category: billing | Last updated: 2026-08-23

## Summary

Add the VAT ID under Billing > Tax details. Valid EU VAT IDs switch future invoices to reverse charge; past invoices can be reissued by support once the ID validates.

## Symptoms

You may see this when VAT was added to my invoice even though we entered our EU VAT ID.

## Common question

Customers often ask how do I add our VAT number so it shows on invoices.

## Resolution

1. Add the VAT ID under Billing > Tax details.

2. Valid EU VAT IDs switch future invoices to reverse charge; past invoices can be reissued by support once the ID validates.
