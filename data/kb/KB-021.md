# Backups and point-in-time restore

Article ID: KB-021 | Category: technical | Last updated: 2026-08-16

## Summary

Point-in-time restore is available for the last 7 days on Pro and 30 days on Enterprise. Use Database > Backups > Restore to a new instance, then swap the connection string.

## Symptoms

You may see this when we accidentally dropped a table and need to restore the database to an earlier time.

## Common question

Customers often ask how far back can I restore a database backup.

## Resolution

1. Point-in-time restore is available for the last 7 days on Pro and 30 days on Enterprise.

2. Use Database > Backups > Restore to a new instance, then swap the connection string.
