# Regional outages and the status page

Article ID: KB-024 | Category: technical | Last updated: 2026-08-13

## Summary

Check status.nimbus.example for active incidents and subscribe to updates. During a regional incident, fail over to a secondary region if multi-region is enabled.

## Symptoms

You may see this when nothing in our us-east region is responding and all services are unreachable.

## Common question

Customers often ask where can I see if there is an outage in a region.

## Resolution

1. Check status.nimbus.example for active incidents and subscribe to updates.

2. During a regional incident, fail over to a secondary region if multi-region is enabled.
