# Billing contacts and receipt emails

Article ID: KB-014 | Category: billing | Last updated: 2026-08-26

## Summary

Add billing contacts under Billing > Contacts. Each contact receives invoices and receipts; check spam filters for messages from billing@nimbus.example.

## Symptoms

You may see this when our accounts team is not receiving any of the receipt emails after payments.

## Common question

Customers often ask how do I add someone in finance to receive invoices.

## Resolution

1. Add billing contacts under Billing > Contacts.

2. Each contact receives invoices and receipts; check spam filters for messages from billing@nimbus.example.
