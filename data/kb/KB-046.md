# Inviting team members and seat limits

Article ID: KB-046 | Category: account | Last updated: 2026-08-28

## Summary

Each plan includes a number of seats. Remove inactive members or buy extra seats under Settings > Members > Seats to send new invites.

## Symptoms

You may see this when I can't invite new team members, the invite button says the seat limit is reached.

## Common question

Customers often ask how many team members can we invite on our plan.

## Resolution

1. Each plan includes a number of seats.

2. Remove inactive members or buy extra seats under Settings > Members > Seats to send new invites.
