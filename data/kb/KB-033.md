# Terraform provider authentication

Article ID: KB-033 | Category: technical | Last updated: 2026-08-25

## Summary

Set NIMBUS_TOKEN in the CI environment with a token that has the infra:write scope. The provider does not read the CLI login session.

## Symptoms

You may see this when the Nimbus Terraform provider fails with 'invalid credentials' in our CI pipeline.

## Common question

Customers often ask how do I authenticate the Terraform provider.

## Resolution

1. Set NIMBUS_TOKEN in the CI environment with a token that has the infra:write scope.

2. The provider does not read the CLI login session.
