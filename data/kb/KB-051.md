# Roles and permissions

Article ID: KB-051 | Category: account | Last updated: 2026-08-10

## Summary

Workspaces have Owner, Admin, Developer and Viewer roles. Only Owner and Admin can access billing; change roles under Settings > Members.

## Symptoms

You may see this when a teammate with the Developer role cannot see the billing page they need.

## Common question

Customers often ask what can each role do in a workspace.

## Resolution

1. Workspaces have Owner, Admin, Developer and Viewer roles.

2. Only Owner and Admin can access billing; change roles under Settings > Members.
