# Duplicate or double charges

Article ID: KB-002 | Category: billing | Last updated: 2026-08-05

## Summary

Duplicate charges are almost always a pending authorisation plus the settled charge. If both settle, support reverses the duplicate within 5 business days; no refund request form is needed.

## Symptoms

You may see this when I was charged twice for the same subscription this month, two identical charges on my card.

## Common question

Customers often ask what happens if a payment is accidentally charged twice.

## Resolution

1. Duplicate charges are almost always a pending authorisation plus the settled charge.

2. If both settle, support reverses the duplicate within 5 business days; no refund request form is needed.
