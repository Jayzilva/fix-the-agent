# Credits and promo codes

Article ID: KB-013 | Category: billing | Last updated: 2026-08-14

## Summary

Enter promo codes under Billing > Credits before the invoice is finalised. Codes are single-use per workspace and cannot be applied to invoices already issued.

## Symptoms

You may see this when the promo code we were given is not applying the discount at checkout.

## Common question

Customers often ask how do I redeem a promo code or account credit.

## Resolution

1. Enter promo codes under Billing > Credits before the invoice is finalised.

2. Codes are single-use per workspace and cannot be applied to invoices already issued.
