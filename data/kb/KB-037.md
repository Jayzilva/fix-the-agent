# Mobile SDK crash on iOS startup

Article ID: KB-037 | Category: technical | Last updated: 2026-08-27

## Summary

SDK 4.x requires iOS 15 or later and must be initialised on the main thread. Update to 4.2.1, which fixes the startup crash on iOS 15 devices.

## Symptoms

You may see this when our iOS app crashes on launch after updating the Nimbus mobile SDK.

## Common question

Customers often ask which iOS versions does the mobile SDK support.

## Resolution

1. SDK 4.x requires iOS 15 or later and must be initialised on the main thread.

2. Update to 4.2.1, which fixes the startup crash on iOS 15 devices.
