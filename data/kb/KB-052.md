# Accessing audit logs

Article ID: KB-052 | Category: account | Last updated: 2026-08-02

## Summary

Audit logs are available on Enterprise under Settings > Audit log and are kept for 1 year. They can be streamed to a SIEM via the audit webhook.

## Symptoms

You may see this when the audit log page is empty for our workspace.

## Common question

Customers often ask how can we see who changed settings in our workspace.

## Resolution

1. Audit logs are available on Enterprise under Settings > Audit log and are kept for 1 year.

2. They can be streamed to a SIEM via the audit webhook.
