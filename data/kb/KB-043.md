# Resetting your password

Article ID: KB-043 | Category: account | Last updated: 2026-08-23

## Summary

Use 'Forgot password' on the sign-in page. Reset links expire after 30 minutes; if no email arrives, check spam and confirm the address with a workspace admin.

## Symptoms

You may see this when the password reset email never arrives so I can't get into my account.

## Common question

Customers often ask how do I reset my password.

## Resolution

1. Use 'Forgot password' on the sign-in page.

2. Reset links expire after 30 minutes; if no email arrives, check spam and confirm the address with a workspace admin.
