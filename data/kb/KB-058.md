# Service level agreement and uptime credits

Article ID: KB-058 | Category: other | Last updated: 2026-08-02

## Summary

Enterprise plans include a 99.95% uptime SLA. Service credits are requested within 30 days of the incident.

## Resolution

1. Enterprise plans include a 99.95% uptime SLA.

2. Service credits are requested within 30 days of the incident.
