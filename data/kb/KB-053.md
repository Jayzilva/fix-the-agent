# Merging two workspaces

Article ID: KB-053 | Category: account | Last updated: 2026-08-21

## Summary

Workspaces cannot be merged automatically. Transfer each project under Project > Settings > Transfer to the target workspace, then delete the empty one.

## Symptoms

You may see this when we have two separate workspaces by mistake and our projects are split between them.

## Common question

Customers often ask can two workspaces be merged into one.

## Resolution

1. Workspaces cannot be merged automatically.

2. Transfer each project under Project > Settings > Transfer to the target workspace, then delete the empty one.
