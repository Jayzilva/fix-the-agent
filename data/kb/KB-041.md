# WebSocket connections dropping

Article ID: KB-041 | Category: technical | Last updated: 2026-08-20

## Summary

Idle connections are closed after 60 seconds. Send a ping frame every 30 seconds to keep WebSocket connections open.

## Symptoms

You may see this when WebSocket connections drop every 60 seconds.

## Common question

Customers often ask does the platform support long-lived WebSocket connections.

## Resolution

1. Idle connections are closed after 60 seconds.

2. Send a ping frame every 30 seconds to keep WebSocket connections open.
