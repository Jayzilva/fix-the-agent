# Transferring workspace ownership

Article ID: KB-047 | Category: account | Last updated: 2026-08-26

## Summary

The current owner transfers ownership under Settings > Workspace > Transfer. If the owner is unavailable, support transfers it after verifying a company domain admin.

## Symptoms

You may see this when our workspace owner left the company and we can't change any owner settings.

## Common question

Customers often ask how do I transfer ownership of a workspace to someone else.

## Resolution

1. The current owner transfers ownership under Settings > Workspace > Transfer.

2. If the owner is unavailable, support transfers it after verifying a company domain admin.
