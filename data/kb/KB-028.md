# Logs missing from the log explorer

Article ID: KB-028 | Category: technical | Last updated: 2026-08-27

## Summary

Logs are retained for 7 days on Pro and 30 days on Enterprise. Missing logs usually mean the app writes to a file instead of stdout/stderr.

## Symptoms

You may see this when the log explorer shows no logs for our service since yesterday.

## Common question

Customers often ask how long are logs kept in the log explorer.

## Resolution

1. Logs are retained for 7 days on Pro and 30 days on Enterprise.

2. Missing logs usually mean the app writes to a file instead of stdout/stderr.
