# Deleting your account and data (GDPR)

Article ID: KB-049 | Category: account | Last updated: 2026-08-10

## Summary

Request deletion under Profile > Privacy. Data is erased within 30 days of the request and a confirmation email is sent when it completes.

## Symptoms

You may see this when I requested account deletion weeks ago and my data is still there.

## Common question

Customers often ask how do I permanently delete my account and personal data.

## Resolution

1. Request deletion under Profile > Privacy.

2. Data is erased within 30 days of the request and a confirmation email is sent when it completes.
