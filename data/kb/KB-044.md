# Lost two-factor authentication device

Article ID: KB-044 | Category: account | Last updated: 2026-08-20

## Summary

Use one of your backup codes to sign in. Without backup codes, a workspace owner can reset 2FA for you, or support verifies identity before resetting it.

## Symptoms

You may see this when I lost my phone and can't pass two-factor authentication to log in.

## Common question

Customers often ask what should I do if I change phones and use two-factor authentication.

## Resolution

1. Use one of your backup codes to sign in.

2. Without backup codes, a workspace owner can reset 2FA for you, or support verifies identity before resetting it.
