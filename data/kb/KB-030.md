# DNS propagation for custom domains

Article ID: KB-030 | Category: technical | Last updated: 2026-08-21

## Summary

DNS changes propagate within the record TTL, usually under an hour. Lower the TTL to 300 seconds before migrating and verify with 'dig' against a public resolver.

## Symptoms

You may see this when our custom domain still points to the old host after we updated DNS.

## Common question

Customers often ask how long does DNS take to update after changing records.

## Resolution

1. DNS changes propagate within the record TTL, usually under an hour.

2. Lower the TTL to 300 seconds before migrating and verify with 'dig' against a public resolver.
