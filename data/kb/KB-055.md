# SCIM user provisioning

Article ID: KB-055 | Category: account | Last updated: 2026-08-25

## Summary

Generate a SCIM token under Settings > SSO > SCIM and paste it into the identity provider. Tokens expire after 1 year and must be regenerated.

## Symptoms

You may see this when SCIM provisioning stopped creating new users from our identity provider.

## Common question

Customers often ask how do I set up SCIM provisioning.

## Resolution

1. Generate a SCIM token under Settings > SSO > SCIM and paste it into the identity provider.

2. Tokens expire after 1 year and must be regenerated.
