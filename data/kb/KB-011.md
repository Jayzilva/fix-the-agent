# Purchase orders and invoicing for Enterprise

Article ID: KB-011 | Category: billing | Last updated: 2026-08-08

## Summary

Enterprise customers can add a PO number under Billing > Invoice settings. It appears on every future invoice; the account manager can reissue past invoices with the PO.

## Symptoms

You may see this when our finance team rejected the invoice because it is missing our purchase order number.

## Common question

Customers often ask can you put our purchase order number on invoices.

## Resolution

1. Enterprise customers can add a PO number under Billing > Invoice settings.

2. It appears on every future invoice; the account manager can reissue past invoices with the PO.
