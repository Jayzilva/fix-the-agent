# Restoring a deleted project

Article ID: KB-039 | Category: technical | Last updated: 2026-08-18

## Summary

Deleted projects are recoverable for 7 days from Settings > Trash. After 7 days the project and its data are permanently removed.

## Symptoms

You may see this when someone deleted one of our projects by mistake and we need it back.

## Common question

Customers often ask can a deleted project be recovered.

## Resolution

1. Deleted projects are recoverable for 7 days from Settings > Trash.

2. After 7 days the project and its data are permanently removed.
