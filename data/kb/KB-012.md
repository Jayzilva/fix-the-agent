# Currency and regional pricing

Article ID: KB-012 | Category: billing | Last updated: 2026-08-11

## Summary

Billing currency is set when the first invoice is created and supports USD, EUR and GBP. Changing currency requires support to migrate the subscription at the next renewal.

## Symptoms

You may see this when we were charged in US dollars instead of euros and our bank added conversion fees.

## Common question

Customers often ask can we be billed in our local currency.

## Resolution

1. Billing currency is set when the first invoice is created and supports USD, EUR and GBP.

2. Changing currency requires support to migrate the subscription at the next renewal.
