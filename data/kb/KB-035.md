# Load balancer health checks failing (502)

Article ID: KB-035 | Category: technical | Last updated: 2026-08-05

## Summary

Health checks call the configured path every 10 seconds and expect HTTP 200 within 5 seconds. Make sure the app listens on 0.0.0.0 and the PORT variable.

## Symptoms

You may see this when the load balancer returns 502 Bad Gateway because health checks are failing.

## Common question

Customers often ask how are load balancer health checks configured.

## Resolution

1. Health checks call the configured path every 10 seconds and expect HTTP 200 within 5 seconds.

2. Make sure the app listens on 0.0.0.0 and the PORT variable.
