# Cancelling a subscription

Article ID: KB-009 | Category: billing | Last updated: 2026-08-01

## Summary

Cancel under Billing > Plan > Cancel subscription. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period, and no further renewals are charged after that date.

## Symptoms

You may see this when I cancelled my subscription but I was still billed for the next month.

## Common question

Customers often ask how do I cancel our subscription.

## Resolution

1. Cancel under Billing > Plan > Cancel subscription.

2. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period, and no further renewals are charged after that date.
