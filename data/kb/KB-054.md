# Suspicious logins and security incidents

Article ID: KB-054 | Category: account | Last updated: 2026-08-03

## Summary

Immediately reset passwords, revoke all sessions and API tokens under Settings > Security, and enable 2FA. Report the incident to security@nimbus.example.

## Symptoms

You may see this when we see logins to our account from a country we have never been to and settings were changed.

## Common question

Customers often ask what should I do if I think my account was accessed by someone else.

## Resolution

1. Immediately reset passwords, revoke all sessions and API tokens under Settings > Security, and enable 2FA.

2. Report the incident to security@nimbus.example.
