# Switching between monthly and annual billing

Article ID: KB-006 | Category: billing | Last updated: 2026-08-20

## Summary

Switch under Billing > Plan > Billing period. Moving to annual starts a new 12-month term today and credits the unused part of the current month.

## Symptoms

You may see this when I tried to switch to annual billing and the switch failed but I was still charged.

## Common question

Customers often ask can I switch my subscription from monthly to annual billing.

## Resolution

1. Switch under Billing > Plan > Billing period.

2. Moving to annual starts a new 12-month term today and credits the unused part of the current month.
