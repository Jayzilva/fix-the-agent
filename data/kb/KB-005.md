# Upgrading and downgrading plans (proration)

Article ID: KB-005 | Category: billing | Last updated: 2026-08-27

## Summary

Plan changes are prorated to the day. Upgrades charge the difference immediately; downgrades issue account credit that is applied to the next invoice.

## Symptoms

You may see this when I upgraded my plan mid-month and got charged a strange partial amount.

## Common question

Customers often ask how is the price calculated when I upgrade or downgrade in the middle of a billing cycle.

## Resolution

1. Plan changes are prorated to the day.

2. Upgrades charge the difference immediately; downgrades issue account credit that is applied to the next invoice.
