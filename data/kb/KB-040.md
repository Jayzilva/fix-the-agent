# Secrets manager access denied

Article ID: KB-040 | Category: technical | Last updated: 2026-08-09

## Summary

Attach the secret to the service under Secrets > Access. Services can read only secrets explicitly granted to them, and grants apply on the next deploy.

## Symptoms

You may see this when our service gets 'access denied' when reading secrets from the secrets manager.

## Common question

Customers often ask how do services get permission to read secrets.

## Resolution

1. Attach the secret to the service under Secrets > Access.

2. Services can read only secrets explicitly granted to them, and grants apply on the next deploy.
