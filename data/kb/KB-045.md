# Configuring SSO with SAML

Article ID: KB-045 | Category: account | Last updated: 2026-08-12

## Summary

Set the Audience URI to https://auth.nimbus.example/saml/<workspace-id> in the identity provider and upload the IdP metadata under Settings > SSO.

## Symptoms

You may see this when SAML single sign-on fails with 'invalid audience' and nobody can log in through our identity provider.

## Common question

Customers often ask how do I set up SAML single sign-on.

## Resolution

1. Set the Audience URI to https://auth.nimbus.example/saml/<workspace-id> in the identity provider and upload the IdP metadata under Settings > SSO.
