# Database connection pool exhausted

Article ID: KB-020 | Category: technical | Last updated: 2026-08-24

## Summary

Each managed Postgres size has a connection limit. Enable the built-in PgBouncer pooler under Database > Pooling and point the app at the pooler port 6543.

## Symptoms

You may see this when our app is throwing 'too many connections' errors from the managed Postgres database.

## Common question

Customers often ask how many database connections does each plan allow.

## Resolution

1. Each managed Postgres size has a connection limit.

2. Enable the built-in PgBouncer pooler under Database > Pooling and point the app at the pooler port 6543.
