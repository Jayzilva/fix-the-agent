# Changing your account email address

Article ID: KB-048 | Category: account | Last updated: 2026-08-19

## Summary

Change the email under Profile > Email. The confirmation link expires after 24 hours and must be opened while signed in.

## Symptoms

You may see this when I changed my email address but the confirmation link says it is invalid.

## Common question

Customers often ask how do I change the email address on my account.

## Resolution

1. Change the email under Profile > Email.

2. The confirmation link expires after 24 hours and must be opened while signed in.
