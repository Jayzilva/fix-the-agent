# Account locked after failed logins

Article ID: KB-050 | Category: account | Last updated: 2026-08-23

## Summary

Accounts lock for 30 minutes after 10 failed attempts. Wait 30 minutes or ask a workspace admin to unlock the account under Settings > Members.

## Symptoms

You may see this when my account is locked after too many failed login attempts.

## Common question

Customers often ask how long does an account lockout last.

## Resolution

1. Accounts lock for 30 minutes after 10 failed attempts.

2. Wait 30 minutes or ask a workspace admin to unlock the account under Settings > Members.
