# Failed payments and the suspension grace period

Article ID: KB-008 | Category: billing | Last updated: 2026-08-07

## Summary

After a failed payment Nimbus retries on days 3, 5 and 7. The workspace is suspended only after the 7-day grace period; paying the open invoice lifts the banner immediately.

## Symptoms

You may see this when our payment failed and now there is a red banner saying the workspace will be suspended.

## Common question

Customers often ask what happens to our workspace if a payment fails.

## Resolution

1. After a failed payment Nimbus retries on days 3, 5 and 7.

2. The workspace is suspended only after the 7-day grace period; paying the open invoice lifts the banner immediately.
