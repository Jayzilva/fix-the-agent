# Object storage 403 AccessDenied

Article ID: KB-022 | Category: technical | Last updated: 2026-08-06

## Summary

Bucket access is controlled by bucket policies and token scopes. Grant the token the storage:write scope and check that the bucket policy does not deny the uploading role.

## Symptoms

You may see this when uploads to our storage bucket fail with 403 AccessDenied.

## Common question

Customers often ask how do bucket permissions work in object storage.

## Resolution

1. Bucket access is controlled by bucket policies and token scopes.

2. Grant the token the storage:write scope and check that the bucket policy does not deny the uploading role.
