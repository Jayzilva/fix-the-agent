# Understanding your Nimbus invoice

Article ID: KB-001 | Category: billing | Last updated: 2026-08-11

## Summary

Invoices list the base plan, prorated plan changes, compute-hour overages and taxes as separate lines. Open Billing > Invoices and click a line to see the usage that produced it.

## Symptoms

You may see this when my invoice shows line items I don't recognise and the total is higher than my plan price.

## Common question

Customers often ask can you explain what each line on my monthly invoice means.

## Resolution

1. Invoices list the base plan, prorated plan changes, compute-hour overages and taxes as separate lines.

2. Open Billing > Invoices and click a line to see the usage that produced it.
