# Deployments failing with 'build timeout'

Article ID: KB-015 | Category: technical | Last updated: 2026-08-20

## Summary

Builds time out after 20 minutes. Enable build caching in Project > Settings > Build and move large asset downloads to a pre-built base image to stay under the limit.

## Symptoms

You may see this when every deployment fails with the error 'build timeout' after 20 minutes.

## Common question

Customers often ask what is the maximum build time for a deployment.

## Resolution

1. Builds time out after 20 minutes.

2. Enable build caching in Project > Settings > Build and move large asset downloads to a pre-built base image to stay under the limit.
